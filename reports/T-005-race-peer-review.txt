The proposed mitigation is sound. Holding the original `CREATE_NEW` handle open with `DELETE` rights and using `FileRenameInfo` for the replacement eliminates the TOCTOU window entirely—the kernel operates on the handle's file object, not the pathname. The same handle can set `FileDispositionInfo` on failure, avoiding the pathname-based `DeleteFileW` in the destructor that the third REPRO test exploits. The existing ACL checks, mutex, and parent-directory handle provide defense in depth but cannot close a window that exists in kernel transition between user-mode calls. The strategy correctly avoids overpromising—same-user tampering within process is inherently limited by what the process can observe, and power-loss atomicity depends on filesystem journaling guarantees outside this code's control.
