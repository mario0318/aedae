# Information architecture

`aedae.tech` is the product, security, and build-status authority. `world`, `life`, `live`, and
`online` are differentiated public surfaces, not independent products.

Each domain must meet a renewal bar at the August 2027 review: world publishes a revised argument
or falsifier; life corrects or adds practical entries; live records at least one real note per
review window; online remains a useful directory. Missing that bar means redirect or lapse is
preferable to an empty brand surface.
